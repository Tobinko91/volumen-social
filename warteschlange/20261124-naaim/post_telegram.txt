📉 <b>Fund managers are dumping stocks</b>

The NAAIM Exposure Index (stock exposure of US active investment managers) fell to 51, its lowest since April 2025. It dropped 36 points (−41%) in two weeks, the biggest 2-week decline since November 2023.

For perspective: the index bottomed at 11 in the 2020 pandemic crash and at 13 in the 2022 bear market.

<b>Our view</b>
The big money is leaving fast, but this is not panic yet. We think the selling is not done.
