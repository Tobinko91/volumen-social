🚨 <b>BREAKING: Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO</b>

Nvidia announced it today, Nov 12, at 8:48am ET (13:48 UTC): Jensen Huang has resigned as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately.

• CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO and stays CFO.
• Harvey C. Jones, former Synopsys CEO, becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair.
• The board is searching for a permanent CEO, inside and outside the company.

<b>What comes next</b>
• Tue, Nov 17, 2pm PT / 22:00 UTC: Nvidia reports Q3 results. All eyes on the first call without Huang.
