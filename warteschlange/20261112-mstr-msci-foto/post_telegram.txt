🚨 <b>BREAKING: MSCI kicks Strategy out of its indexes</b>

MSCI confirmed it on Wednesday, Nov 11, after the US close, in its November index review: Strategy (MSTR), Michael Saylor's company, leaves MSCI USA and with it MSCI World and ACWI. Metaplanet and the UK uranium holder Yellow Cake are removed too. Effective at the US close on Monday, Nov 30 (4pm ET / 21:00 UTC).

<b>What it means</b>
• Index funds hold about 13 million Strategy shares, 3.1% of the company. They have to sell them.
• Strategy pays for its Bitcoin by selling new shares. Index funds took every new share automatically. From December, that buyer is gone.

<b>What comes next</b>
• Until Nov 30: index deletions are usually traded ahead of time, the funds sell in the closing auction.
• Nov 30, 4pm ET / 21:00 UTC: the closing auction in New York.
• From December: Strategy's weekly Monday filings show if new money still goes into Bitcoin.
• February: next MSCI review. Nearly two dozen crypto and holding firms are on the watchlist.
