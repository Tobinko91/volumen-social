🔎 <b>Fact check: a viral post says the market is doomed. Real or panic?</b>

✅ Real: Democrats won the House and the Senate on Nov 3.
✅ Real: the US-Iran war is in its seventh month, Brent trades near $100.
🟡 Half: "yields are exploding". The 10-year US yield rose to 5.16% on Nov 13, the highest since October.
❌ Wrong: "Nvidia −40% in a few months". It closed 27% below its Oct 6 peak on Nov 11.
❌ No proof: "Japan and China will dump US Treasuries next week". There is no such report.
❌ Panic: "the biggest crash in history has officially begun", "99% will lose everything". No data behind it.

<b>Our view</b>
Panic posts exaggerate, but the risk is real: a Fed hike priced in for December, yields above 5%, oil near $100, billions leaving bitcoin ETFs. We think a broader selloff across stocks, bonds and crypto has become more likely. That is an assessment, not a forecast.
