⚠️ <b>iPhone + crypto: one ad can be enough</b>

Security firm Confiant tracked a hacker group running ads in 47 countries, across more than 480 ad domains since May. On a vulnerable iPhone, simply opening the page behind an ad can trigger the attack.

An invisible frame reads your iOS version and picks one of four exploit kits. They are built to take wallet seed phrases, saved passwords, messages and photos.

At risk: iOS 18 or older, with wallets like MetaMask, Phantom or Trust Wallet. The flaw is in iOS, not in the wallet apps.

<b>Do this today</b>
• Update to iOS 26
• Can't update? Turn on Lockdown Mode
• Never keep your seed phrase in Notes, Photos or screenshots
• Big amounts: hardware wallet

Share this with someone who holds crypto on an iPhone 👇
