🧊 <b>While everyone watched Nvidia: 3 stories about who controls your money</b>

1. Paraguay, Nov 3: the central bank seized Ueno Bank, a digital bank tied to President Santiago Peña. About 3 million customers, around 60% of all adults, could not withdraw or transfer money on day one.
2. Binance, Oct 29: $6.3 billion stolen, the biggest crypto heist on record. The FBI blames North Korea.
   Within a day, Tether and Circle froze more than $2.37 billion of the stolen stablecoins. No judge involved, courts came later.
3. Washington, Oct 21: the OCC finalized the GENIUS Act rules. From Jan 18, 2027, US banks can issue their own payment stablecoins.
   Congress just flipped from red to blue, but the law passed with both parties: 68–30 in the Senate, 308–122 in the House.

<b>Our view</b>
A digital dollar isn't cash. It can be frozen with one click, for hackers and for everyone else. Safer money, or more control?
