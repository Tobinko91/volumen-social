🚨 <b>Microsoft. Nvidia. Now the banks.</b>

Monday, Nov 16: thousands of customers of Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and U.S. Bank reported they could not log in or move money. Downdetector showed over 4,600 reports for Chase, 1,900 for Bank of America, 900 for U.S. Bank and 800 for Wells Fargo.
Chase called it a technical issue, not a cyberattack. Bank of America and U.S. Bank gave no explanation. No bank has said when everything will be fully fixed.

<b>Last week</b>
Nov 12: Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang resigned. Nov 13: a global Microsoft outage hit Outlook, Teams and Azure.

<b>Our view</b>
Tech giants are shaking, banks are going dark, and on Jan 18 US banks may start issuing their own digital dollars. We think the old system is starting to collapse, and a new one is being built right now. Time will tell.
