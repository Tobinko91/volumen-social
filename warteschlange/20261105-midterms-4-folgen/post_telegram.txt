🗳 <b>Midterms, part 4: what a Democratic Congress means for crypto</b>

<b>Power</b>
Democrats won control of the House and the Senate on Nov 3. The new Congress takes over on Jan 3, 2027.

<b>Who writes the rules</b>
• Elizabeth Warren is expected to lead the Senate Banking Committee. She has pushed for tougher consumer protection and anti-money-laundering rules for crypto.
• Maxine Waters is the top Democrat on the House Financial Services Committee.

<b>CLARITY Act (crypto market rules)</b>
• Passed the House in July 2025, failed a Senate vote 49–50 in September. If it is not passed by Jan 3, it dies.
• One side: “likely dead in the water in its current form” (security firm executive, Nov 4).
• The other side: more than 70 House Democrats voted for it. A new bill in 2027 is possible.

<b>The money</b>
Crypto political groups spent about $170.5 million in this election, at least $125.5 million of it on Republicans.

<b>Stablecoins</b>
Banks and crypto firms still disagree on stablecoin rewards. Banks fear deposits could leave bank accounts.
