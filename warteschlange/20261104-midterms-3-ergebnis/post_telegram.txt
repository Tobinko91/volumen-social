🗳 <b>Democrats take Congress. Bitcoin dipped, then bounced.</b>

Results of Tue, Nov 3: Democrats won control of the House and the Senate. A few Senate races are still being counted.

<b>Election night, minute by minute</b>
• <b>10:10 PM ET</b> (03:10 UTC): BTC low $75,740, a 7-week low
• <b>10:15 PM ET</b> (03:15 UTC): CBS calls the House for Democrats
• <b>11:40 PM ET</b> (04:40 UTC): By then: AP calls the Senate for Democrats
• <b>1:16 AM ET</b> (06:16 UTC): BTC back near $77,900 · ETH ~$2,300 (+2%)

<b>Why crypto cares</b>
• Crypto trades 24/7, so Bitcoin reacted first, overnight. The low came minutes before the House call. Once the result was clear, the price recovered.
• The CLARITY Act (crypto market rules) passed the House 294–134 in July 2025. In the Senate it failed a vote 49–50 on Sep 15. It needs 60.
• If the current Senate does not pass it by Jan 3, it dies and has to start over in the new Congress.
• From Jan 3, Democrats chair the Senate Banking and House Financial Services committees. Their top Democrats today: Elizabeth Warren and Maxine Waters.
• President Trump keeps his veto. New crypto laws need both Congress and the White House.
