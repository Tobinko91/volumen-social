🚨 <b>Jobs beat: +165K vs 60K expected</b>

US October jobs report, Nov 6, 8:30 AM ET (13:30 UTC):

• Jobs added: +165,000. Expected: about 60,000. September: 60,000.
• Unemployment rate: 4.1% (expected 4.2%, September 4.2%).
• Average hourly earnings: +0.4% on the month (expected +0.3%).
• Revisions: September raised from 29,000 to 60,000, August from 133,000 to 186,000.
• Wages are up 3.1% from a year ago.

<b>What comes next</b>
The Fed decides on rates on Dec 9 at 2:00 PM ET (19:00 UTC). Before the data, markets already saw a December rate hike as likely. Strong hiring and faster wage growth give the Fed less reason to wait.
