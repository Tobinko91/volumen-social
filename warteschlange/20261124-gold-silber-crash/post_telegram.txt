📉 <b>Gold below $3,600, silver below $45. Why now?</b>

1. Inflation came in hotter than expected, so the Fed is expected to hike again in December.
2. Higher rates: US bonds pay more, gold and silver pay nothing. Money moves into bonds.
3. The strong dollar makes metals more expensive for the rest of the world.
4. The war with Iran pushed oil and inflation higher.
5. The AI selloff: margin calls force investors to sell what they can. Gold and silver get sold first.

History: gold crashed in 2008 and 2020 and recovered within a few years. After 2011 it took much longer.

<b>Our view</b>
As long as the Fed keeps hiking, the pressure is not over yet.
