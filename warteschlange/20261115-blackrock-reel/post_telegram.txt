🔎 <b>Real or panic? "BlackRock is dumping hundreds of millions in BTC and ETH"</b>

A viral post shows BlackRock's IBIT and ETHA wallets sending 1,884.9 BTC ($213.7M) and 59,606 ETH ($254.5M) to Coinbase Prime, plus 900 BTC to other addresses.

<b>Do the math</b>
$34.01M ÷ 300 BTC = $113,367 per Bitcoin. $42.69M ÷ 10,000 ETH = $4,269 per Ether. Bitcoin is nowhere near that today, so the screenshot is old.

<b>And even if it were new</b>
Coinbase is the custodian of BlackRock's ETFs. When investors redeem ETF shares, coins move to Coinbase Prime. That is a redemption, not BlackRock "dumping" on its own.

<b>What is real</b>
Nearly $3 billion left US spot bitcoin ETFs from Oct 30 to Nov 13. That is the real pressure, and it shows up in the official flow data.
