⚠️ <b>Trust Wallet users: be careful</b>

For weeks, Trust Wallet users have been reporting empty wallets. On-chain investigators traced over $5.4 million in USDT drained from reported wallets between Oct 5 and Nov 11. The biggest single loss: 601,752 USDT on Nov 7, from a wallet untouched since 2021.

CZ warned about a fake Trust Wallet site (“.cam is not .com”). For the other drains the cause is unknown. Trust Wallet has not confirmed a flaw. Many victims were on iPhones.

<b>Our advice</b>
• Big amounts: move them to a hardware wallet
• Update your phone
• Revoke old token approvals
• Never type your seed phrase on any website
