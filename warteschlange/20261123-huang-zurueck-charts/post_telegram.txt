🔁 <b>Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia CEO</b>

Monday, Nov 23: Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, President and board member, effective immediately. 11 days after he resigned, and 6 days after the chair said the board did not intend to bring him back.

The board says it received “input from senior levels of the United States government” and that his return poses no national security risk. Trump had said Nvidia should take him back. Colette Kress goes back to CFO.

Huang: “I am honored to return to NVIDIA.”
