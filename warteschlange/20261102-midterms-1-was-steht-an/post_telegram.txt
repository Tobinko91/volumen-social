🗳 <b>US midterms on Tuesday: what's at stake for crypto</b>

On Tue, Nov 3, Americans elect all 435 House seats and about a third of the Senate. Republicans control both chambers today.

<b>Why crypto cares</b>
• The crypto industry has spent more than $200 million on the 2026 races.
• The CLARITY Act, the bill that would split crypto oversight between the CFTC and the SEC, passed the House 294–134 in July 2025. On Sep 15, 2026, it failed a key Senate vote 49–50. It needs 60 votes.
• Sen. Dave McCormick (R-Pa.) after the vote: "This issue isn't going away." Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.) says the US needs crypto rules, but not a bill written by the industry.

<b>What the markets expect</b>
• Prediction markets on Nov 1: Democrats 94% to win the House, 72% to win the Senate.

<b>What comes next</b>
• First polls close Tue 6:00 PM ET (23:00 UTC). The Senate could be clear around midnight ET (05:00 UTC Wed), the House can take days.
• Crypto trades 24/7, so it is the first market to react overnight. The new Congress starts Jan 3.
