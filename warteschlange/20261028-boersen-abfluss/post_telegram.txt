📰 <b>14,942 BTC left exchanges in one day</b>

Santiment counted a net outflow of about 14,942 BTC from exchanges on Oct 27, the largest daily outflow in five weeks.

• It came one day before the Fed decision
• Bitcoin traded near $80,800 on Wednesday morning
• Outflows can mean coins move to self-custody
• They can also be custody changes or moves between platforms
